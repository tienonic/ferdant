What we look for

Independent businesses that test, maintain and repair the electrical equipment of utilities and plants.

The work

  • Acceptance and maintenance testing of electrical power equipment
  • Transformer testing, service and repair
  • Switchgear and circuit breaker service
  • Protective relay testing and calibration
  • Motor and generator repair

The customers

Electric utilities and municipal systems, water and irrigation districts, food processors, manufacturers and other plants that cannot afford an outage.

The business

  • Owner-run, with a crew that customers ask for by name
  • Repeat work from the same customers year after year
  • A record of safe work
  • In California or elsewhere in the West to begin with

The owner

Someone thinking about the next ten years: a retirement, a partner who wants to step back, or a wish to hand the business to an owner who will keep it whole.

If this sounds like your business, start with a conversation.
