Privacy

You do not need an account to read these pages.

Tools work in your browser

Search terms, bill totals, change breakdowns, cancellation selections and message-builder notes are used in the open page only. This site does not send them to a server, save them to local storage or set tracking cookies. The site keeps no saved copy of tool inputs. Copying a template uses your clipboard only after you press the copy button.

Choosing a city and downloading

City choices and service searches stay in the browser. An explicit city download fetches a standard offline file and adds your chosen city locally. It includes the city and its saved contact chart, without copying private tool notes or the service filter. The file remembers the city chosen at download; later choices in an open file last until it closes.

Hosting and outside links

The public site uses GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Reddit and agency links lead to services with their own policies.

No document uploads

There is no upload form here. Keep identity documents, account numbers, credentials and case records out of public discussions. Use a verified private process when the task requires those details.
