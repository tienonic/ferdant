Paperwork guide

Ask about a charge

US consumer guidance · Sources checked November 2, 2026

What to do

Name one issue, give the important dates and ask for one specific next step.

  1. Say which charge or account step is unclear.
  2. Give the key dates and any previous reference.
  3. Say what answer or correction you are asking for.
  4. Use verified support for private account details.
Copy a starting point

A support message does not replace a formal dispute, appeal or required notice.

Build a billing message

One issue, a short timeline and a specific request. Skip account numbers and identifying details.

Your draft

I’m writing about [the charge or issue]. Please explain the charge and the rate or term behind it. I can provide supporting records through your verified secure channel.

Nothing is sent or saved here. This draft does not replace a formal dispute, appeal or required notice.

Message template

Replace the brackets with your details. Send through the business’s verified support channel.

I am contacting you about [one issue] relating to [service or transaction]. On [date], I [relevant action]. Your response on [date] said [what was confirmed]. The remaining question is [question]. I am asking for [specific next step]. I have kept [supporting records] and can provide them through your secure channel.

Step-by-step detail

01Name one issue+

Start with the service or transaction and the part that needs explanation. You may need the reason for a fee, a cancellation status or a missing confirmation. Separate unrelated issues so each response can be tracked.

02Build a short timeline+

In private notes, list important dates, what you requested and what response arrived. Connect each statement to a record you hold. An automated acknowledgment is different from a completed correction. Check an unresolved request’s reference before filing it again.

03Ask for a concrete next step+

Say what would answer the question: an explanation, a copy of a term or a correction you believe is needed. The FTC’s sample letter recommends a concise account, your requested outcome and supporting copies. Keep the originals. Save the reply alongside the original request.

04Choose the right destination+

Use a verified business support route for private account details. For a formal complaint, check the agency’s scope and prerequisites. The CFPB handles specified consumer financial products, not every utility, telephone or service complaint. Check the status of a previous complaint before submitting again.

Sources & scope

An initial inquiry does not replace a formal dispute, appeal or other required notice. Check official requirements and deadlines. Share account identifiers only through a verified private channel.
