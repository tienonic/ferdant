Questions for the community

What do you need help with?

  • Why is this bill different from the last one?
  • How do I cancel and keep a record?
  • What should I include when asking for a correction?

Include when you ask

  • The type of bill, term or form you are trying to understand
  • What you expected and what the document actually says
  • The relevant dates and the one step that is blocked
  • A specific question, with personal details removed

Account decisions and documents stay with you and the responsible organization. These resources organize a question; they do not provide legal, tax or investment advice.
