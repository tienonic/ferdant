## Equipment and services

- Acceptance testing before power equipment enters service and maintenance testing once it is in use
- Transformer testing and repair
- Switchgear maintenance and circuit breaker testing
- Protective relay testing and calibration
- Motor rewinding and generator repair

## What we ask about

Which jobs bring customers back? Which tests can the crew perform with its own instruments? What work goes to another shop? We look at service records and ask who schedules outages, supervises fieldwork and signs test reports.
