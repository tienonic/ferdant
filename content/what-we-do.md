## The specific thing

Utilities and plants run on transformers, switchgear, relays and motors. Small independent shops test that equipment, repair it and sign the report that says it is safe to run. We are buying one such shop in California, and then the next.

## Why it matters

When a transformer or a breaker fails, a plant stops or a neighbourhood goes dark, and a replacement can take a long time to arrive. So the equipment already in service has to be tested, repaired and kept running.

Many of the people who know how are close to retirement, and many of their shops have no one to hand the business to.

## How we own

- **One owner for decades.** {{name}} is not a fund with a date by which it must sell. We intend to hold each business, reinvest what it earns and build it slowly.
- **The shop stays the shop.** A testing or repair shop is its technicians and the customers who ask for them by name. The name on the truck, the crew and the way the work is done stay.
- **Technicians first.** The scarce thing in this trade is a qualified technician whom a customer trusts to sign the report. We judge a business by the people who do the work and by whether customers call them back.
- **AI for the desk, people for the field.** We run our own office work with AI tools. That keeps the office small, so money and attention go to crews, training and test equipment.

## What we look for

- Acceptance and maintenance testing of electrical power equipment
- Transformer testing, service and repair
- Switchgear and circuit breaker service
- Protective relay testing and calibration
- Motor and generator repair

The customers are electric utilities and municipal systems, water and irrigation districts, food processors, manufacturers and other plants that cannot afford an outage.

The business is owner-run, with a crew customers ask for by name, repeat work year after year and a record of safe work. We are starting in California and the West.

## How we are structured

{{name}} is the parent company. Each business we buy stays its own company with its own name, manager and crew. The parent owns the businesses, sets the standard for safety and reporting, and decides where earnings go.

## Where we are today

We are at the start. We have not bought a business yet. We are meeting owners, technicians and the people who buy this work, and we would rather take longer than buy the wrong shop.
