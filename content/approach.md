## One owner for decades

{{name}} is not a fund with a date by which it must sell. We intend to hold each business we buy, reinvest what it earns and build it slowly.

## The shop stays the shop

A testing or repair shop is its technicians and the customers who ask for them by name. The name on the truck, the crew and the way the work is done stay.

We want to add what an owner working alone rarely has time for: hiring and training the next technicians, new test equipment, and help with the paperwork.

## Technicians first

The scarce thing in this trade is a qualified technician whom a customer trusts to sign the report. We judge a business by the people who do the work and by whether customers call them back.

## AI for the desk, people for the field

We run our own office work with AI tools: research, records, scheduling and drafts. That keeps the office small, so money and attention go to crews, training and equipment.

## How we are structured

{{name}} is the parent company. Each business we buy stays its own company with its own name, manager and crew. The parent owns the businesses, sets the standard for safety and reporting, and decides where earnings go.

## Where we are today

We are at the start. We have not bought a business yet. We are meeting owners, technicians and the people who buy this work, and we would rather take longer than buy the wrong shop.
