## What we are doing, specifically

We are buying one independent electrical testing and repair shop in California, and then the next, to own for decades. These shops keep the transformers, switchgear and motors of utilities and plants in service.

The work right now is finding the right shops and earning a conversation with the people who built them: by letter, by telephone and in person.

## Who should apply

People who care that this equipment keeps running and that these shops stay good. If that does not interest you, the work will feel like lists and telephone calls. If it does, you will see what the lists are for. We would rather hire nobody than hire someone who does not understand what we are doing.

We also want people who already work with AI every day and have built something of their own with it. A project you can link tells us more than a grade. We care that you check what the machine tells you, write plainly and finish what you start.

## How hiring works

1. Send an email to [{{contact_email}}](mailto:{{contact_email}}) with the role in the subject line.
2. Include four things: a link to one or two things you built or run; a few lines on how you use AI in your own work (your setup, one thing you made with it and how you checked it); a resume or LinkedIn page; and three or four sentences on why you want to do this work, in your own words and written without AI.
3. If it looks like a fit, a twenty-minute call with the founder.
4. Then {{trial}}, scored against a written standard you see in advance.
5. If the trial goes well, you start that week with a written list of what to deliver in your first two weeks.

## The terms
