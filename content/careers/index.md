## Why this work

The country is using more electricity, the equipment that carries it is ageing, and the technicians who know it are retiring. The businesses that test and repair that equipment are small, local and hard to replace.

We are building a company to own them for decades. The work right now is finding the right shops and the people who know them. It is a great deal of careful work, and we want a few people who are fast and exact.

## Who we want

People who already work with AI every day and have built something of their own with it. A project you can link tells us more than a grade. We care that you check what the machine tells you, write plainly and finish what you start.

## How hiring works

1. Send an email to [{{contact_email}}](mailto:{{contact_email}}) with the role in the subject line.
2. Include three things: a link to one or two things you built or run, a few lines on how you use AI in your own work (your setup, one thing you made with it and how you checked it), and a resume or LinkedIn page.
3. If it looks like a fit, you get {{trial}}.
4. If the trial goes well, you start that week.

## The terms

Paid by the hour: {{intern_pay}}, and {{builder_pay}} for the AI Operations Builder, set by your trial. {{intern_hours_cap}}. Davis students and recent graduates preferred. Most of the work is remote; some is in person in Davis and on site visits. You must be authorized to work in the United States. Apply even if you do not meet every line of a role.
