## About the role

The founder's most useful hours are conversations with shop owners, technicians and the people who buy testing work. Today the number of those conversations is limited by how many invitations go out and how well they are followed up. You own the calendar. Each week you put more of the right people in front of him, with a one-page card that says who they are and what we need to learn. Everything else the company does depends on these conversations.

## What you will do

- Keep the contact tracker current: who was asked, what they said and what we owe them next
- Write invitations and follow-ups, and telephone the front desk to find the right person and a time
- Book video calls of 20 to 30 minutes and site visits, then confirm, remind and rebook
- Write a one-page card before each call: who the person is, what they have seen first-hand, and the one question we need answered
- After each call, record what was said, what was promised and who follows up
- Make short fact-finding calls yourself to purchasing desks, trade associations and licensing boards

## You may be a good fit if you

- Write a clear three-sentence email on the first try
- Are comfortable telephoning a stranger and asking for ten minutes
- Keep a list in order without being asked
- Use AI to draft and research, and check its work before it goes out
- Have held a job where you dealt with customers: sales, service, canvassing, recruiting

## Strong candidates may also

- Have booked speakers or sponsors for a club
- Have worked in sales development or fundraising
- Come from a family in the trades or in farming
- Speak Spanish

**Useful backgrounds.** Communication, Managerial Economics, Economics, Political Science. Any major if the description fits you.

## Your first two weeks

- Take over follow-up on the open conversations
- Book the December site visits
- Have five new calls on the calendar by the end of the second week
