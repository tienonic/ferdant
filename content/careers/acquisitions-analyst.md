## About the role

Before we can buy a good shop we need to know every shop: who owns it, how long it has run, who its customers are, how many certified technicians it has and whether the owner is near a handover. Much of that is public, in licence records, accreditation lists, bid results and purchase orders released by utilities and cities. You build and keep this map, and you find the documents that show who paid whom for what. The first purchase will be chosen from your list, and every letter starts from your notes on the shop.

## What you will do

- Build the list of independent testing, transformer service and motor repair shops in California and the West from public sources
- Record for each shop the owner, the year it started, crew size, certifications and named customers, each with its source
- Find the one true detail about each shop that a letter can open with
- Pull public purchasing records for testing and maintenance work: bid results, awarded contracts and purchase orders
- File public records requests where the record is not posted
- Watch for shops that are already for sale, and bring forward the best ten shops each week

## You may be a good fit if you

- Are careful in a spreadsheet and bothered by a number without a source
- Enjoy digging through filings and directories until the answer turns up
- Use AI to pull and structure information, then verify it against the original
- Write short, factual summaries
- Have taken courses in economics, accounting or statistics

## Strong candidates may also

- Have built a dataset or a scraper of their own
- Have filed public records requests, for a student paper or a campaign
- Read a balance sheet comfortably

**Useful backgrounds.** Economics, Managerial Economics, Statistics, Accounting, Political Science.

## Your first two weeks

- A complete, sourced list for Northern California
- Ten one-page shop profiles
- Three public purchasing records found and summarized
