## Business Development Intern {#business-development}

Research individual shops and draft a letter about each one for review and signature. Deliver letters in the Sacramento area, Stockton, Modesto and the Bay Area. Call only when asked, to identify the right contact or arrange a time. Schedule and confirm owner conversations and visits.

Before each meeting, write a one-page brief on the owner, the shop’s services and the question to ask. Update the contact record the same day.

You need a car and a driver’s license for local visits. Customer-facing experience, clear handwriting and the ability to check AI-assisted research against sources are useful.

## Acquisitions Analyst Intern {#acquisitions-analyst}

Identify independent shops using the NETA and EASA directories, California contractor licenses and company filings. Record each shop’s ownership, services, crew size and certifications. Link each fact to a source and flag information you cannot verify.

Use utility and municipal bid awards and purchase orders to identify customers. Request public records when needed. Flag shops listed for sale and recommend ten shops each week for closer review.

In the first two weeks, build a Northern California shop list, write ten shop profiles and summarize three purchasing records. The role involves spreadsheet research and concise writing. Coursework in economics, accounting or statistics is useful.

[form] [Apply](#apply)

## Engineering Intern, Power Equipment {#engineering}

Read sample test reports, equipment specifications and bid requirements. Explain what measurements such as insulation resistance, transformer turns ratio and breaker contact resistance tell a customer. Use ANSI/NETA ATS for acceptance testing and ANSI/NETA MTS for maintenance testing as references.

For each shop, identify the equipment it tests, the instruments and qualifications it lists, and the work it sends elsewhere. Prepare technical questions for owner calls and take notes on shop visits.

The first assignments are a guide to ten common tests, capability summaries for five shops and questions for December visits. Coursework in circuits, electric machines or power systems will help.

[form] [Apply](#apply)

## AI Automation Intern {#ai-automation}

Write Python or JavaScript tools to collect shop directory entries and extract fields from public bids and purchase orders. Keep a source link with each record. Compare a sample of extracted fields with the originals and report how often they disagree.

In the first two weeks, build collectors for two public directories, measure errors on one set of purchasing documents and document how another intern can run the tools.

Bring an example of a tool you built with Claude Code, Codex or Cursor and a task you divided among several AI agents. Be ready to explain how you checked their results.
