Operating Partner

Help us choose the first business, then lead it as its president.

About the role

We are looking for a senior person from the electrical testing and field service trade to help us choose our first business and then lead it as its president. Customers and technicians already know you and want to work with you. You have run a shop, a branch or a region, and you know what separates a crew that gets called back from one that does not. This is the most important hire we will make.

What you will do

  • Look at candidate shops with us: the crew, the customers, the safety record and the equipment
  • Introduce us to owners, technicians and buyers you trust
  • Help shape the offer and the plan for the first year after a purchase
  • Lead the business after we buy it, and recruit and keep its technicians

You may be a good fit if you

  • Have fifteen or more years in electrical testing, field service or utility maintenance
  • Have run a shop, a branch or a region and answered for its results
  • Have had technicians follow you from one job to the next
  • Are known to buyers at utilities and plants in the West
  • Say plainly what you think

Strong candidates may also

  • Hold NETA Level III or IV certification or a professional engineer's licence
  • Have sold a shop, or brought one into a larger company
  • Have built a safety record you are proud of

Logistics

A paid advisory contract to start. When we buy a business, the president's role, with pay and ownership tied to its results. Location in California or the West.

How to apply

Write to Nicholas Melnichenko at nicholas@ferdant.com with a few lines on what you have run. He replies himself, and the first step is a conversation.
