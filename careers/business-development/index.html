Business Development Intern

Earn the founder a conversation with shop owners, by letter, telephone and visit.

About the role

The founder's most useful hours are conversations with shop owners, technicians and the people who buy testing work. Shop owners get form letters from buyers and throw them away. A letter written by hand by someone who knows what the shop does gets read, and a call from someone who has been to the door gets taken. You earn those conversations. Everything else the company does depends on them.

What you will do

  • Write letters by hand to shop owners, each one about that owner's shop, and deliver some of them in person
  • Telephone the front desk a week later to find the right person and a time
  • Write short emails and follow-ups where a letter is not the right first step
  • Book video calls of 20 to 30 minutes and visits, then confirm, remind and rebook
  • Write a one-page card before each call: who the person is, what they have seen first-hand, and the one question we need answered
  • Keep the record: who was asked, what they said, what we owe them next
  • Make short fact-finding calls yourself to purchasing desks, trade associations and licensing boards

You may be a good fit if you

  • Write a clear three-sentence note on the first try, and write it neatly by hand
  • Are comfortable walking into a shop or telephoning a stranger and asking for ten minutes
  • Have a car and a licence, and do not mind an afternoon driving between shops in the valley
  • Keep a list in order without being asked
  • Use AI to research a shop before you write, and check its work
  • Have held a job where you dealt with customers: sales, service, canvassing, recruiting

Strong candidates may also

  • Have booked speakers or sponsors for a club, or raised money
  • Come from a family in the trades or in farming
  • Speak Spanish

Useful backgrounds. Communication, Managerial Economics, Economics, Political Science. Any major if the description fits you.

Your first two weeks

  • Twenty-five handwritten letters delivered or posted, each with a telephone follow-up
  • The open conversations taken over and every one answered
  • Five new calls on the founder's calendar

Logistics

Part-time, paid by the hour: $20 to $24 an hour, set by your trial. 10 to 15 hours a week during the quarter, lighter through finals, more over winter break (December 12 to January 3) if you want it. Davis preferred. Starts with a paid trial task of about three hours on real work. You must be authorized to work in the United States.

How to apply

Email nicholas@ferdant.com with the subject "Business Development Intern - Your name". Send a link to one or two things you built or run; a few lines on how you use AI in your own work (your setup, one thing you made with it and how you checked it); a resume or LinkedIn page; and three or four sentences on why you want to do this work, in your own words and written without AI.
