Work at Ferdant

Join at the start. Paid, part-time roles in Davis, and conversations with senior people from the trade.

Why this work

The country is using more electricity, the equipment that carries it is ageing, and the technicians who know it are retiring. The businesses that test and repair that equipment are small, local and hard to replace.

We are building a company to own them for decades. The work right now is finding the right shops and the people who know them. It is a great deal of careful work, and we want a few people who are fast and exact.

Who we want

People who already work with AI every day and have built something of their own with it. A project you can link tells us more than a grade. We care that you check what the machine tells you, write plainly and finish what you start.

Open roles: interns

  • AI Operations BuilderBuild the tools the rest of the team runs on.
  • Outreach and Scheduling AssociatePut the right people on the founder's calendar every week.
  • Research Analyst, AcquisitionsMap every shop in the trade and find the records that show who paid whom for what.
  • Technical Research Intern, Power EquipmentExplain the work: what each test proves and what a crew needs to do it.

Open roles: senior people from the trade

  • Operating Partner, Electrical Testing and Field ServiceHelp us choose the first business, then lead it.
  • Technical Advisor, Senior Test TechnicianLook at shops with us and tell us what you see.

How hiring works

  1. Send an email to nicholas@ferdant.com with the role in the subject line.
  2. Include three things: a link to one or two things you built or run, a few lines on how you use AI in your own work (your setup, one thing you made with it and how you checked it), and a resume or LinkedIn page.
  3. If it looks like a fit, you get a paid trial task of about three hours on real work.
  4. If the trial goes well, you start that week.

The terms

Paid by the hour: $20 to $24 an hour, and $24 to $30 an hour for the AI Operations Builder, set by your trial. 10 to 15 hours a week during the quarter, lighter through finals, more over winter break (December 12 to January 3) if you want it. Davis students and recent graduates preferred. Most of the work is remote; some is in person in Davis and on site visits. You must be authorized to work in the United States. Apply even if you do not meet every line of a role.

Not on the list?

If you think you can help and no role fits, write anyway and say what you would do in your first two weeks.

Write to nicholas@ferdant.com
