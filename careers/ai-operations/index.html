AI Operations Builder

Build the tools the rest of the team runs on.

About the role

Most of our desk work is done with AI agents: research, list building, pulling facts out of documents and drafting. That is how a few people can cover a whole trade. You build and look after the tools the rest of the team uses, and you turn anything we do twice into something that runs by itself. Every hour you save the others goes to conversations and visits.

What you will do

  • Build collectors for public sources such as licence boards, accreditation directories and purchasing portals
  • Turn bid documents and filings into structured rows, each with a link to its source
  • Set up checks that catch wrong or invented output before anyone relies on it
  • Keep the shared workspace of sheets, files and scripts in order and documented
  • Teach the rest of the team the workflows that work

You may be a good fit if you

  • Use Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or similar tools every day and have built things with them that you can show
  • Write Python or JavaScript
  • Care more about whether the output is right than whether the demo is impressive
  • Can explain what you built to someone who does not code

Strong candidates may also

  • Run agents or automations of their own
  • Have scraped and cleaned real data
  • Have contributed to an open-source project

Useful backgrounds. Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Cognitive Science. Any major if your projects make the case.

Your first two weeks

  • A working collector for two public directories
  • One document pipeline with a measured accuracy check
  • A short guide the others can follow

Logistics

Part-time, paid by the hour: $24 to $30 an hour, set by your trial. 10 to 15 hours a week during the quarter, lighter through finals, more over winter break (December 12 to January 3) if you want it. Davis preferred. Starts with a paid trial task of about three hours on real work. You must be authorized to work in the United States.

How to apply

Email nicholas@ferdant.com with the subject "AI Operations Builder - Your name". Send a link to one or two things you built or run, a few lines on how you use AI in your own work (your setup, one thing you made with it and how you checked it), and a resume or LinkedIn page.
