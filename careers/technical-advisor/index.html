Technical Advisor, Senior Test Technician

Look at shops with us and tell us what you see.

About the role

We want an experienced test technician or shop foreman to look at shops with us and tell us what they see. A few hours a week, paid by the hour. You have spent years in substations and plants and can tell from a test report, a truck and a tool crib whether a crew does careful work.

What you will do

  • Read sample test reports and tell us what is good and what is missing
  • Come on site visits and look at equipment, calibration records and safety practice
  • Tell us what a job should take in hours, people and equipment
  • Help us write the questions we ask owners and technicians

You may be a good fit if you

  • Have ten or more years of field testing on transformers, switchgear, breakers or relays
  • Hold or have held NETA Level III or IV certification, or equivalent experience
  • Have trained younger technicians
  • Are working, semi-retired or retired, and would enjoy a few paid hours a week

Logistics

Paid by the hour at a rate we agree in the first conversation. A few hours a week, mostly by telephone and video, with site visits in Northern California.

How to apply

Write to Nicholas Melnichenko at nicholas@ferdant.com with a few lines on your experience.

Apply by emailnicholas@ferdant.com
