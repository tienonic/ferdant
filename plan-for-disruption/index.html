Questions for the community

What do you need help with?

  • How would we contact each other if service went down?
  • What does our household need to plan around?
  • What worked, what failed and what should change?

Include when you ask

  • The disruption and general location you are planning for
  • Access, transport or other needs without identifying details
  • The part of the plan you have actually checked
  • The unresolved step and the official guidance you are using

Follow current instructions from local authorities during an incident. Community discussion is not a live alert service or emergency response.
