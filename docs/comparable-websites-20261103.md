# Ferdant comparable websites by audience and financial scale

Research date: November 3, 2026.

Findhelp has the largest disclosed investment found in this comparison; Citizens Advice has the largest verified annual organizational income; iFixit has the highest estimated monthly website traffic among the four domains with traffic estimates below. These findings cover the selected comparables, not every resource website.

This comparison supports choices about which services to study for Ferdant. Website visits, individual users, outreach reach, investment and annual income are separate measures. No combined popularity-and-money score is justified by the available evidence.

## Website popularity

The earlier comparison produced this provisional order from indexed September 2026 traffic estimates. Counts cover entire domains, not just the tools most similar to Ferdant, and measure visits rather than unique people.

| Provisional rank | Website | September estimate | Relevant feature |

| 1 | [iFixit](https://www.semrush.com/website/ifixit.com/overview/) | 14.92 million visits | Repair instructions and practical troubleshooting |
| 2 | [USA.gov](https://analytics.explodingtopics.com/website/usa.gov) | 11.6 million visits | Government services and benefits navigation |

**Evidence limitation:** only iFixit's opened report matched the indexed September figure. Opened versions of the other reports returned USA.gov at 10.3 million visits in July 2026, Findhelp at 3.64 million in January 2026, and CFPB at 5.1 million in August 2026. The September order therefore remains an indexed estimate, not a verified same-month comparison. In particular, the available evidence does not securely establish Findhelp's position relative to CFPB. Traffic estimates are third-party measurements, not audited counts supplied by the organizations.

## Investment and public funding

**Findhelp leads on disclosed investment in this set.** TPG announced an approximately **$250 million investment on March 3, 2026**. This transaction amount is not annual revenue, and the announcement does not establish that all proceeds were new operating capital. Current annual revenue was not verified. [TPG announcement](https://www.tpg.com/news-and-insights/tpg-partners-with-findhelp-to-expand-access-to-essential-services-for-underserved-populations)

[California budget summary](https://www.dgs.ca.gov/-/media/Divisions/OSP/Publications/FinalBudgetSummary_25-26_Final.pdf), [Cal OES program information](https://www.caloes.ca.gov/office-of-the-director/policy-administration/finance-administration/grants-management/community-resiliency-listos-grants/listos-california-grant-program/)

## What to study for Ferdant

The comparison suggests studying **Findhelp for city-based resource discovery, Citizens Advice for clear explanations, and Watch Duty for emergency information**. iFixit is a useful reference for actionable repair instructions. This is an editorial priority, not a measured prediction of Ferdant's future demand or revenue.

Keep **211 California as the source benchmark for local emergency-alert signup links**. Its relevance to the city/county feature does not depend on its traffic rank. Local automation should choose the reviewed registration link for the selected place; enrollment remains on the official provider's website.

This document preserves the research results and their material limitations. It does not change the resource directory, enroll anyone in alerts or establish a schedule for refreshing these figures.
