What we do

We acquire independent electrical testing and repair shops and keep them whole under long-term ownership.

Englebright Dam, Yuba River

What the shops do

Utilities, water districts and plants run on transformers, switchgear, circuit breakers, protective relays, motors and generators. Independent shops test each piece, repair it and sign the report that says it is safe to energize.

A typical job is a weekend shutdown at a plant: a crew tests every breaker and transformer on the site, and the plant restarts on Monday with a signed report.

Why it matters

  • Demand is rising. Electricity use in the United States rose about 7% from 2019 to 2024, after a decade of decline. Spending on factory construction nearly doubled in three years, and more than 125 gigawatts of data centers are planned.
  • The equipment is old. More than half of the distribution transformers in service, about 40 million of them, are over 33 years old.
  • A replacement is years away. In 2025 the average wait for a new power transformer in the United States was 128 weeks, about two and a half years.
  • Testing is required in writing. Since January 2023 the national maintenance standard, NFPA 70B, requires a written maintenance program, tests on a schedule, and records. For fifty years before, it was a recommendation.
  • The shops are small. An independent testing or motor repair shop is often still run by its founder. When the founder retires, the choices are a sale to a large national group, a sale to the employees, or closing.

Sources: Wood Mackenzie, "Mind the gap", October 2025 · National Fire Protection Association on NFPA 70B

How we own

  • Long-term ownership. Ferdant is not a fund with a date by which it must sell. We hold each shop and reinvest what it earns.
  • The shop stays the shop. The name on the truck, the crew and the way the work is done stay.
  • Technicians first. The scarce thing in electrical testing is a certified technician whom a customer trusts to sign the report. We judge a shop by its technicians and by whether customers call them back.
  • AI for the desk, people for the field. We run our own office work with AI tools, so money goes to crews, training and test instruments.

What we look for

  • Acceptance and maintenance testing of electrical power equipment
  • Transformer testing, service and repair
  • Switchgear and circuit breaker service
  • Protective relay testing and calibration
  • Motor and generator repair

Customers: electric utilities and municipal systems, water and irrigation districts, food processors, manufacturers and other plants that cannot afford an outage.

The shop is owner-run, with repeat work from the same customers and a record of safe work. We are starting in California and the West.

How we are structured

Ferdant is the parent company. Each shop stays its own company with its own name, manager and crew. The parent owns the shops, sets the standard for safety and reporting, and decides where earnings go.

Where we are going

Rising electricity demand, new factories and data centers are the large movements of the next twenty years. Ferdant intends to take part in them as a long-term owner: first electrical testing and repair shops, then other industrial service businesses that utilities and plants depend on.

Where we are today

We are meeting owners, technicians and the people who buy testing and repair work, to choose our first shop. We will take longer rather than buy the wrong one.
