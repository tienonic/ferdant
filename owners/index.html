For owners

A conversation first. No broker, no pitch, and nothing you tell us goes further.

What the first conversation covers

  • What your customers come back for
  • Who runs the work day to day, and who would run it if you stepped back
  • What you would want the next owner to keep

What we will tell you

Who we are, what we are looking for, how we would pay for a business and what would stay the same.

If your shop is not a fit, we will say so, and we would still be grateful for what you can teach us about the trade.

How to reach us

Write to Nicholas Melnichenko, founder, at nicholas@ferdant.com. A few lines about your business are enough. He replies himself.
