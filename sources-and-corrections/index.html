Sources & corrections

A useful guide makes it easy to check a claim and flag what needs another look.

What the dates mean

We link the original publisher where possible. The publisher’s date and our source-check date are different: a recent check does not make an older source newly published. Missing source dates remain explicit.

What these pages can support

These are original practical summaries and working tools, not a republished archive of community conversations. Calculations state their assumptions.

Suggest a correction

Send the page URL, exact statement, reason it may be wrong and a supporting public source to the relevant community’s moderator mail. Do not include private documents or account access. Reddit may require sign-in. Review timing is not promised.

Check what applies

Check the source, location and service before relying on a page. Material corrections should record what changed and when.
